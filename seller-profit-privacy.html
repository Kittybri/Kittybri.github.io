Seller Profit
privacy policy.

Effective September 3, 2026

What the app stores

Seller Profit Calculator stores calculations you choose to save, including item names, marketplace selections, purchase costs, sale prices, shipping, packaging, advertising, fee values, and calculated results. This information stays in the app’s private local storage on your device.

Data collection and sharing

ScaraHandler does not collect, transmit, sell, or share the calculations you enter. The app has no ScaraHandler account system, analytics SDK, advertising SDK, or cloud database.

CSV export

If you export saved calculations, Android’s system document interface lets you choose where to create the CSV file. The export is written to that user-selected location. ScaraHandler does not receive a copy.

Seller Profit Pro and Google Play Billing

If you start, restore, or manage a Seller Profit Pro subscription, Google Play processes the transaction and provides the app with purchase and entitlement information needed to unlock Pro. Google handles payment and account information under Google’s privacy policy. ScaraHandler does not receive full payment-card details and does not operate a server that stores purchase tokens.

Data deletion

You can delete individual saved calculations inside the app. Uninstalling the app removes its locally stored data, but does not automatically cancel an active subscription. Subscriptions can be managed or canceled in Google Play. ScaraHandler has no server copy of your calculations to delete.

Children

Seller Profit Calculator is a general-audience business utility intended for adults and is not designed for children. It does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes and contact

If a future version changes how data is handled, this policy will be updated before that version is released. Privacy or support questions can be sent to abrianakit@yahoo.com.
