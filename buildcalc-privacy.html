BuildCalc
privacy policy.

Effective September 3, 2026

What BuildCalc stores

BuildCalc stores calculator favorites and estimates you choose to save, including project or client labels, estimate names, measurements, waste percentages, unit prices, material categories, and calculated results. This information stays in BuildCalc’s private local storage on your device. Avoid entering sensitive information into project labels.

Data collection and sharing

ScaraHandler does not collect, transmit, sell, or share your projects, measurements, prices, or estimates. BuildCalc has no ScaraHandler account system, analytics SDK, advertising SDK, or cloud database.

PDF export

If you export a project estimate, Android’s system document interface lets you choose where to create the PDF. The PDF is written to that user-selected location. ScaraHandler does not receive a copy.

BuildCalc Pro and Google Play Billing

If you start, restore, or manage a BuildCalc Pro subscription, Google Play processes the transaction and provides BuildCalc with purchase and entitlement information needed to unlock Pro. Google handles payment and account information under Google’s privacy policy. ScaraHandler does not receive full payment-card details and does not operate a server that stores purchase tokens.

Data deletion

You can delete saved estimates inside BuildCalc. Uninstalling BuildCalc removes its locally stored data, but does not automatically cancel an active subscription. Subscriptions can be managed or canceled in Google Play. ScaraHandler has no server copy of your project data to delete.

Children

BuildCalc is a general-audience construction utility intended for adults and is not designed for children. It does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes and contact

If a future BuildCalc version changes how data is handled, this policy will be updated before that version is released. Privacy or support questions can be sent to abrianakit@yahoo.com.
